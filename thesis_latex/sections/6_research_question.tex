\section{Research Questions}
\label{sec:proposed_approach}

The decomposition of Section~\ref{subsec:decomposition} and the bound of Section~\ref{sec:theory} make a set of falsifiable predictions about how the $\lambda$-curriculum and its refinements should behave. We first evaluate these predictions on the rotated MNIST toy dataset with a small multilayer perceptron, before investigating whether the findings hold when scaling to a deeper backbone and a more demanding generalization setting. For this, we employ Domain CIFAR-10, which represents a substantially harder task that better approximates the complexities of real-world applications. The experiments are organised around three primary questions, the second of which carries four sub-questions probing the individual ingredients of the curriculum.

\paragraph{RQ1 --- The cause.} Does the stability gap decompose into the magnitude, estimator, and trajectory contributions of Section~\ref{subsec:decomposition}, and is the discontinuity their dominant source?

\paragraph{RQ2 --- The fix.} Does removing the discontinuity with the $\lambda$-curriculum reduce the stability gap without final-accuracy cost? Four sub-questions examine how:
\begin{description}
    \item[RQ2a (linear ramp).] Does the linear curriculum reduce gap depth monotonically as the ramp length $N$ grows?
    \item[RQ2b (adaptive schedule).] Does an adaptive schedule achieve the same reduction while removing the explicit ramp-length knob $N$?
    \item[RQ2c ($\lambda_{\min}$ floor).] Does a small floor $\lambda_{\min} > 0$ recover early-task learning speed without re-introducing the stability gap?
    \item[RQ2d (momentum).] Does momentum damp the depth fluctuation that remains after the curriculum is applied, yet do nothing when used on its own, since the very first step after a task boundary is deterministic and carries no momentum from the new task?
\end{description}

\paragraph{RQ3 --- Generalisation.} Do these findings carry over to a deeper backbone (ResNet-18) and a stronger task shift (CIFAR-10 domain corruption)?
